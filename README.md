## 📌 About Me &middot; درباره من

I am **Shayan Shirazi (شایان شیرازی)**, an embedded systems and firmware engineer specializing in low-level hardware bring-up from raw silicon to operating system services. My expertise centers on designing multi-layer industrial PCBs in **Altium Designer**, writing custom **U-Boot bootloaders**, bringing up **Linux kernel drivers**, and reverse engineering firmware binaries.

من **شایان شیرازی** هستم؛ طراح سخت‌افزار و مهندس سیستم‌های تعبیه‌شده (Embedded Linux & Firmware). تمرکز اصلی من طراحی مدارهای صنعتی در آلتیوم، توسعه بوت‌لودرهای پیشرفته لینوکس، درایورهای کرنل و پلتفرم‌های شبکه OpenWrt است.

* 🔌 **Hardware Architecture:** End-to-end schematic capture, high-speed multi-layer PCB layout, signal integrity, and SMD assembly.
* ⚡ **Firmware & Kernel:** Custom bootloaders (`U-Boot`), device tree overlays, embedded Linux (`OpenWrt`), and Linux kernel modules.
* 📡 **IoT & Gateways:** MediaTek `MT7628` / `HLK-7628N`, `ESP32-S3/C3`, dual-SIM LTE modem sequencing, shift registers, and RS485/UART subsystems.
* 👾 **Binary & Hardware Security:** Firmware extraction, SPI sniffing, in-circuit unbricking, and reverse engineering.
