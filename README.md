## 📌 About Me

I architect, route, solder, and bring hardware to life from raw silicon up to user-space services. My craft lives at the bare-metal and kernel boundaries: designing custom multi-layer PCBs in **Altium Designer**, writing modular **U-Boot bootloaders**, bringing up Linux drivers, and unraveling firmware binaries.

When I’m not probing SPI busses with logic analyzers or optimizing bootloader assembly, you’ll find me meticulously profiling espresso shots or competing in hardware/binary CTFs.

* 🔌 **Hardware Architecture:** End-to-end schematic capture, high-speed multi-layer PCB layout, signal integrity, and SMD assembly.
* ⚡ **Firmware & Kernel:** Custom bootloaders, device tree overlays, embedded Linux (`OpenWrt`), and Linux kernel modules.
* 📡 **IoT & Gateways:** MediaTek `MT7628` / `HLK-7628N`, `ESP32-S3/C3`, dual-SIM LTE modem sequencing, shift registers, and RS485/UART subsystems.
* 👾 **Binary & Hardware Security:** Firmware extraction, SPI sniffing, in-circuit unbricking, and reverse engineering.
