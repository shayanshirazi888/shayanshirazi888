## 📌 About Me

I design, route, assemble, and bring custom hardware to life from raw silicon up to kernel drivers and user-space applications. Beyond hardware and embedded systems, I am an **active CTF competitor** who regularly dives into Capture The Flag tournaments, reverse engineering binaries, dissecting firmware, and tackling low-level security challenges.

Parallel to code and circuit boards is an uncompromising passion for **specialty coffee**. I am endlessly fascinated by the physics and chemistry of coffee extraction—profiling pressure curves, dialing in espresso grind sizes, and pulling precision shots. There is almost always a fresh cup of espresso next to my logic analyzer and terminal.

* 👾 **Active CTF Competitor:** Regularly playing CTF tournaments; focused on Reverse Engineering (Rev), Binary Exploitation (Pwn), Hardware Hacking, and firmware analysis.
* ☕ **Espresso & Specialty Coffee:** Deeply obsessed with artisanal coffee extraction, dialing in beans, roast profiling, and sipping meticulous brews throughout every project.
* 🔌 **Hardware Architecture:** End-to-end schematic capture, high-speed multi-layer PCB layout in Altium Designer, signal integrity, and manual SMD soldering.
* ⚡ **Firmware & Kernel:** Custom bootloaders (`U-Boot`), device tree overlays, embedded Linux (`OpenWrt`), and Linux kernel modules.
* 📡 **IoT & Gateways:** MediaTek `MT7628` / `HLK-7628N`, `ESP32-S3/C3`, dual-SIM LTE modem sequencing, shift registers, and RS485/UART subsystems.
